Start here: the 2-minute setup

  1. Open the app link. There's no login screen and nothing to sign up for — it just opens.
  2. You'll see starter cards already in place (a sample routine, a sample week, a few sample tasks). They're examples, not assignments — edit or delete anything that isn't you.
  3. Tap around. Nothing here can break — the worst case is you delete a card you wanted, and you can re-add it in seconds.
The whole system comes from Sidetracked Home Executives: you're not messy, you're sidetracked. The goal was never a perfect house — it's a house that runs on small, repeatable routines. Done is better than perfect.

Today: your daily flow

The Today screen is home base. Here's the rhythm:

1. Today’s cards — all equal

Everything lined up for today lives in one uniform list — no card gets VIP treatment. Tap the circle to check one off; tap a title (or press and hold) to edit it right here. The slim header up top keeps score: your completion percentage, minutes finished, and cards done. Like things in a certain order? Tap ⇅ Order above the list to arrange your cards with the arrow buttons, then tap ✓ Done to lock it in. When family members are set up, chips above the list let you view one person's day, a few people's, or everyone's — the scores up top follow whoever's selected.

2. Refile without guilt

Can't do it today? Tap Refile and send it to Tomorrow, Later this week, or Next week. Refiling isn't failing — it's the system working. The book's rule: re-file, don't pile.

Tip: Each card shows how many minutes it takes. If you only have 15 minutes, pick a 15-minute card. Small wins count.
  • To-dos: catch it, don't lose it. Think of something mid-day? Tap ＋ Add a to-do at the bottom of the Today tab — just a title and minutes, no scheduling. It lands on today's deck and rolls into tomorrow automatically until you check it off. To-dos live only here, in the day view — they don’t clutter up the card box. Tap any card's title — or press and hold the card — to edit it right here in the day view. No need to hunt through the card box. Need a to-do off your list without losing it? Park it until tomorrow, later this week, or next week — parked to-dos wait in the 🅿 Parked list at the bottom of the Today tab and pop back on their own. You can also pull one back early or delete it there.

    • Routines: the autopilot

    Routines are the small things you do every day without thinking — the book's "daily routine" card. There are two to start: Morning and Evening. This tab is the one home for all daily repeating tasks — nothing here appears anywhere else, so you never track the same chore twice.

    • This tab is for building routines — add steps, rename, assign, reorder. Checking off happens on the Today tab and the Board, where each routine sits collapsed until you tap it.
    • Routines reset fresh every morning — yesterday is forgiven, today is a clean card.
    • Tap Add inside any routine to add your own steps. Edit or delete steps anytime.
    • Prefer your routines in a certain order? Tap ⇅ Order at the top of the tab, arrange them with the arrows, then tap ✓ Done.
    • Want a whole new routine — an Afternoon reset, a Sunday tidy? Tap ＋ New routine at the bottom of the tab, name it, then add steps. Tap the ✏️ on any routine to rename it, edit its steps, or delete the whole routine.
    Tip: Keep routines short — 5 to 8 steps. If a routine feels heavy, move steps out to regular cards.

    The Card Box: your digital 3×5 file

    Everything in this app lives on a card, filed under five dividers — just like the book's index-card box. (Daily repeating tasks live in the Routines tab instead — one home for them, so nothing ever shows up twice.)

    • Basic Week — the weekly plan (see below)
    • Day — a one-off job planned for a specific date
    • Monthly — once-a-month jobs
    • Zones — room-by-room deep-clean lists
    • Menus — the weekly meal plan

    Adding a card

    There's an Add button on every list and every day. Tap it, give the card a title, and pick its divider — the form asks only what that kind of card needs. A Basic Week card asks which day of the week; a Monthly card asks which day and which week of the month (the 2nd Tuesday, say); a Zones card asks which week of the month its zone comes up; a Day card asks for the date it's planned for; a Menus card just takes the basics. Add a room and how many minutes it takes if you like — then it's filed and scheduled.

    Editing a card

    Tap any card to edit it. Change the title, timing, schedule, or notes — or delete it. The starter cards are yours to rewrite.

    One account for the whole family

    Every device starts with its own private lists. To share one home across everyone's phones and computers, create a family login:

    1. On the device that has everything (your cards, routines, family members), go to Settings → Family account and tap Create family login. Enter an email and password. That device's lists become the shared family lists.
    2. On every other device, open the app and sign in with that same email and password.

    From then on, everyone sees the same lists, and anything anyone checks off or adds shows up everywhere.

    Which device first matters: always create the login on the device that has your real lists — creating makes that device's lists the shared ones. Other devices should join (Settings → Family account → Join existing login, or sign in from the welcome screen). When a device joins, the app first downloads a backup file of that device's own lists, then switches it to the family lists — lists that were only on that device are not merged in.

    If someone opens the app on a brand-new device, they'll see a welcome screen: sign in with the family login, or continue without an account to keep lists on that device only.

    The board: everyone's day at a glance

    The Board tab lines up each family member's day side by side, like cards on a table. Each column shows their cards, their routines, and their progress — check things off right there and everything stays in sync.

    • On a phone, swipe sideways through the columns; on a bigger screen they sit side by side.
    • Cards with no owner show in every column — they're household jobs.
    • The ＋ To-do button at the bottom of a column adds a to-do for that person.

    Family: whose card is it?

    When the household shares the work, cards can belong to a person. Add everyone in Settings → Family — just names, no logins needed.

    • When you create or edit a card or to-do, pick who it belongs to — or leave it as Everyone for shared jobs.
    • Each person's cards show a small tag with their name, so it's clear at a glance whose is whose.
    • Removing someone turns their cards back into shared household cards — nothing is lost.
    • Routines can belong to someone too — set it when you create or edit a routine, and it shows on their day.
    • The card box always shows everyone's cards, and the week view lines everyone's cards up together day by day, name tags and all.

    The Basic Week: one focus per day

    The book's big idea: instead of marathon cleaning days, give each weekday one focus. The whole house gets covered across the week without any single day feeling huge.

    • Open the Week tab to see the plan, Sunday through Saturday — every day's cards are shown right underneath it.
    • Each day has its own ＋ Add button to file new cards there.
    • Tap the ✏️ on any card to edit it — including moving it to a different day.
    • Make it yours: change focuses, move cards between days, add new ones. The sample week is a starting suggestion, not a rule.

    Progress: kind counting

    The Progress screen tracks two gentle numbers: your routine streak (days in a row the routines got done) and cards done this week. No punishments, no red warnings — just a quiet record that the system is working.

    History: what got done, day by day

    Below the numbers, History shows what was checked off each day — the cards and to-dos finished, the routine steps completed, and the day's totals. Use the ‹ › arrows to look back at earlier days.

    Checked something off by mistake? On today's list, tap Undo next to it and it goes right back on your list — even a to-do that disappeared comes back. Past days are just for looking.

    Your data: what to know

    • It saves by itself. Everything goes to your private database as you go — nothing to remember, nothing to lose.
    • It works offline. No signal? Keep going — it syncs when you're back online.
    • Back up now and then. In Settings there's Export, which downloads all your data as one file. Save one every month or so — it's your safety net. Import puts it back.
    • One browser, one profile. There's no password login — the app recognizes this browser, so each browser or device keeps its own copy. If you switch to a brand-new phone or a different browser, use Export on the old one and Import on the new one to carry everything over — otherwise the new one starts fresh.

    Parental PIN: kid-proofing the delete button

    In Settings → Parental PIN you can set a 4–6 digit PIN. Once it's on, the app asks for it before anything destructive: deleting a card or a routine, removing a family member, importing a backup, or erasing everything. Everyday use — checking things off, adding cards — never asks.

    The PIN belongs to the family account, so it protects every signed-in device at once. Three wrong tries cancels the action. Forgot it? The PIN prompt has a Forgot your PIN? link — enter the family account password and the PIN comes off, so you can set a fresh one.

    Tip: Bookmark the app to your home screen and it opens like a regular app.

    Troubleshooting

    The app shows a "not connected" screen

    It can't reach the database. Check your internet connection and reload. If it persists, the setup may be incomplete — ask whoever set it up for you.

    Cards aren't showing on another device

    Give it a moment to sync, then pull to refresh. Both devices need internet at least once for the sync to happen.

    You deleted something by accident

    Just re-add it — or restore from your latest Export file in Settings.

    Something looks wrong or confusing

    Note what you tapped and what you expected, and pass it along — the app is meant to be shaped around you, so feedback is the feature.

    Your first-week checklist

    • Open the app and look through the starter cards
    • Edit the Morning routine to match your actual morning
    • Set up the Basic Week focuses for your house
    • Add three real To Do cards
    • Do one full Today flow: Up Next → Done → next card
    • Refile something without guilt, just to feel how it works
    • Run your first Export backup in Settings
    Remember: done is better than perfect. The system works if you work it — a little, daily, without guilt.
